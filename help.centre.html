Questions related to Donations

  • Can I claim a tax rebate on my donations?

    Yes, you can claim a tax rebate on donations over a certain threshold. A receipt will be emailed to you instantly.

  • How can I ensure my money goes directly to on-the-ground action?

    We publish transparent financial breakdowns annually, ensuring the vast majority of funds go straight into community projects.

  • How do I claim this credit?

    Submit your official donation receipts through your local tax authority portal at the end of the financial year.

Questions related to how to join the community

  • How do I find local environmental groups?

    Use our interactive regional map tool or sign up for our newsletter to get connected with local coordinators.

  • How can I take climate action in my own backyard?

    Start by planting native flora, composting organic waste, and reducing single-use plastic consumption.

  • Can kura (school) get support for environmental education projects?

    Absolutely. We offer tailored educational resources, toolkits, and potential grant funding for school-led initiatives.

Questions related to the clothes made of reusable plastic

  • Can I recycle these clothes when I'm done?

    Yes, we run a take-back programme where worn garments are broken down and spun into new apparel lines.

  • Is this better than buying natural or second-hand?

    Second-hand is always excellent, but when buying new, repurposing ocean plastics keeps non-biodegradable waste out of landfills.

  • What about chemical safety?

    All material undergoes strict non-toxic processing treatments to ensure it meets international skin safety standards.

Questions related to The research we do

  • Where does this data come from?

    Our data is sourced from collaborative, peer-reviewed studies alongside regional environmental monitoring networks.

  • Does this advice incorporate indigenous knowledge?

    Yes, our methodology deeply integrates traditional ecological knowledge (mātauranga) with contemporary scientific data.

  • How do we know that the information given by the research is credible?

    Every research milestone is evaluated by independent environmental scientists and verified indigenous practitioners before release.
